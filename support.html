01 · Getting started

First launch Defaults Quick start

What does KoreVision do out of the box?

On first launch, KoreVision seeds every calculator with physics-realistic defaults (water/air at room temperature, atmospheric pressure) so you can immediately see non-trivial values. The Home screen shows live Re, Eo, We and Ca badges that update as you change inputs anywhere in the app.

Do I need an account?

No. KoreVision does not require sign-in. Open the app and use it.

Does KoreVision need the internet?

No. Every calculation runs locally on your device. There are no analytics calls, no remote configurations, no fonts loaded over the network.

02 · Calculators

Inputs Units Formatting

Which units does KoreVision use?

All calculators are SI: kg, m, s, K, Pa, N/m, W, J. There is no imperial toggle because almost all multiphase-flow literature is published in SI.

What number format should I pick?

Use Adaptive (default) for everyday work — it switches between fixed and scientific notation based on magnitude. Pick Scientific when comparing exponents, Fixed when showing tables, or Log when you want to see log10 of positive values directly.

Can I save my inputs?

Yes. Every change is persisted in UserDefaults the moment you move off the field, so the next launch restores your last values.

How do I reset inputs back to defaults?

Settings → Data → Reset all inputs to defaults. A confirmation alert appears before any value is wiped.

03 · Regime map

Mandhane Usl vs Usg

What does the regime map show?

The regime map is a horizontal two-phase flow map based on Mandhane's correlation: liquid superficial velocity Usl on one axis, gas superficial velocity Usg on the other. Each cell is coloured by the flow regime (bubbly, slug, churn, annular, mist, dispersed) predicted for that operating point.

Is the regime map a measurement?

No. It is a textbook idealisation drawn from the Mandhane-style classification. Real plant behaviour depends on fluid properties, pipe inclination, geometry and inlet conditions that the map does not encode. Use it as a quick orientation, not as a design certification.

04 · 3D Flow Studio

3D Parameter sweep

What can I do in the 3D Flow Studio?

Pick any three dimensionless numbers (default: Re, Eo, We) and explore the operating envelope as a 3D point cloud. Tap a point to see which flow regime applies at that location.

Can I rotate the view?

Yes. Use the standard iOS gesture — drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, two-finger drag to pan. The view state persists between launches.

05 · Regime Analyzer

Probe Sweep

How do I probe a specific operating point?

Open the Analyzer tab, pick a calculator, and read the live regime tags at the bottom of the detail card. The same tag appears in the Library tab as a coloured pill.

Can I sweep an input and watch the regime transition?

Yes. Drag any input slider slowly — the result label and regime pill update in realtime. Look for the boundary where the pill flips from one colour to the next to locate regime transitions.

06 · Settings & data

Privacy Cache Haptics

What does "Clear cached values" do?

It deletes the local snapshot PNGs (in Documents/snapshots/) and clears the Process Twin sample buffer. Your numeric inputs and preferences are kept — those are governed by "Reset all inputs to defaults".

How do I disable haptic feedback?

Settings → Units & Formatting → toggle off Haptic feedback. The toggle is global, so all feedback generators across the app go silent immediately.

How do I disable pulse animations?

Settings → Appearance → toggle off Pulsing indicators. KoreVision immediately scrubs in-flight breathing animations off every visible layer so the UI matches the new preference without a relaunch.

Does KoreVision sync anything to iCloud?

No. The app does not use iCloud or CloudKit. Your data lives only on the device it was created on.

07 · Troubleshooting

NaN Display Crash

A result shows "—" instead of a number. What does that mean?

The app displays "—" when the computed value is non-finite (∞ or NaN). This usually means a divisor was zero — for example, calling the Power Number with zero density, zero speed, or zero impeller diameter. Provide non-zero values for every input that appears in a denominator.

The screen seems too dim / too small on my device.

KoreVision is designed for iPhone portrait mode and respects Dynamic Type and accessibility text-size settings. If labels look cramped, increase your system text size in Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size.

I think I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please use the contact channel below. Include your device model, iOS version (Settings → General → About), the screen you were on, and what you tapped. Crash details are reported by the operating system itself and are not under the developer's purview.
