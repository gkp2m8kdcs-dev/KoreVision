Privacy Policy

KoreVision is built around a simple promise: we collect nothing about you. Everything the app does happens locally on your device. This page explains, in plain English, exactly what the app stores, what it never stores, and what rights you have.

01 · Summary

  • No personal data is collected, transmitted, sold, or shared.
  • No account or sign-in is required to use any feature.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are embedded.
  • All calculation results are produced on your device and never leave it.
  • You can wipe everything KoreVision stores with one tap in Settings, or by deleting the app.

02 · Data we collect

KoreVision does not collect personal data from you. The only information the app retains lives in your device's local storage and is used solely to make the calculator remember your inputs between launches.

Concretely, the app stores:

  • Numeric inputs you enter for each dimensionless-number calculator (e.g. density, velocity, length, viscosity, surface tension, heat-transfer coefficient). These are stored in your device's UserDefaults under the key korevision.inputs.
  • App settings such as your SI-units preference, haptic-feedback toggle, pulse-animation toggle, and number-format mode (adaptive / scientific / fixed / log). These are stored under standard UserDefaults keys prefixed with settings..
  • Optional snapshot images — only if you explicitly choose to save a snapshot from the Library or Analyzer screen. Snapshots are written to your app's local Documents/snapshots directory and are never exported or transmitted automatically.

None of the above is associated with your identity, Apple ID, Apple ID email, device identifier, advertising identifier (IDFA), or any other personal attribute. There is no remote database that mirrors this content.

03 · Data we never collect

To be unambiguous, KoreVision does not collect:

  • Your name, email, phone number, address, or contact details.
  • Your precise or coarse location.
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, or camera data.
  • Health, biometric, or fitness information.
  • Purchase history, financial information, or payment data.
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioural profiles.
  • Crash logs, diagnostic dumps, or device fingerprints.

04 · Local storage details

Everything KoreVision persists is stored locally using Apple-provided APIs. The app does not use iCloud, CloudKit, or any third-party sync service.

DataMechanismRetention
Calculator inputsUserDefaults key korevision.inputsUntil you tap "Reset all inputs to defaults" in Settings, or delete the app.
User preferences (SI, haptics, pulse, number format)UserDefaults keys settings.*Until you toggle them or delete the app.
Optional snapshot PNGsLocal file in app Documents/snapshots/Until you tap "Clear cached values" in Settings, or delete the app.

If you change devices or reinstall the app, this data is not transferred; it lives only on the device it was created on.

05 · Analytics & tracking

KoreVision contains no analytics SDKs — no Firebase, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no Adjust, no AppsFlyer, no Facebook SDK. The app does not perform any background telemetry, A/B testing, or remote configuration calls.

The app does not display advertising and does not integrate the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA) or the Limit Ad Tracking signal in any way.

Crash and performance diagnostics, where provided by the operating system itself, are governed by Apple's own privacy controls and are not under the developer's purview.

06 · Third-party services

KoreVision does not embed any third-party SDKs or call any third-party services at runtime. There are no fonts loaded from CDNs, no telemetry endpoints, no remote feature flags, no authentication brokers.

The only network access the app makes is the App Store receipt validation performed by Apple itself when you install or update the app, which is handled entirely by the operating system outside of the app's code.

07 · Children's privacy

KoreVision is an engineering reference tool. It is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, this policy applies equally to all users regardless of age.

08 · Your rights & controls

Because KoreVision collects no personal data in the first place, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on your behalf. However, you retain full control over what is stored locally:

  • Reset all inputs: Settings → Data → "Reset all inputs to defaults". This erases every numeric input and restores the seeded defaults on next launch.
  • Clear cached values: Settings → Data → "Clear cached values". This wipes any optional snapshots and the in-memory Process Twin buffer.
  • Delete the app: Removing KoreVision from your device deletes every file and preference it created. Nothing is retained on Apple's servers.
  • Disable haptics: Settings → Units & Formatting → toggle off "Haptic feedback".
  • Disable pulse animations: Settings → Appearance → toggle off "Pulsing indicators".

09 · International users

KoreVision does not transfer any user data across borders, because it transfers none at all. The app is designed to comply with the spirit of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), the UK Data Protection Act, and equivalent regimes by virtue of collecting nothing identifiable.

If you are a regulator, auditor, or enterprise security reviewer and need a data-flow diagram, the answer is one box: your device.

10 · Changes to this policy

If this policy is ever updated, the change will be reflected here with a revised "Last updated" date and shipped with the next release of KoreVision. Material changes that affect what is collected (which today is nothing) would also be surfaced in the Settings screen and the App Store release notes.

11 · Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the data-handling behaviour of KoreVision, please reach the developer through the App Store listing for this app — the "Support" or "Developer Website" link on the product page is the official channel of contact.

For App Store privacy-label disclosures: KoreVision declares "Data Not Collected" in every category.
